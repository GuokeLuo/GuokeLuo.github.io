I am a senior undergraduate student in the School of Traffic & Transportation Engineering at Central South University (CSU), graduating in June 2027.

My research focuses on Humanoid Robotics, Whole-Body Control (WBC), Physics-Informed Reinforcement Learning, and Sim-to-Real Transfer on bipedal platforms such as the Unitree G1. Previously, I conducted research collaborations with mentors at the CMU Safe AI Lab and HKUST.
